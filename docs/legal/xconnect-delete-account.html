Delete your XConnect account

This page explains how to delete your XConnect account and the data linked to it. It applies to the XConnect app for Android and iOS, provided by [COMPANY LEGAL NAME].

Option 1: delete it in the app (fastest)

  1. Open XConnect and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm. If you signed in a while ago, the app may ask you to sign in again first, as a security check.

Your account is deleted straight away, and you are signed out.

Option 2: request deletion by email, without the app

If you no longer have the app installed, or cannot sign in:

  1. Send an email to [PRIVACY CONTACT EMAIL] from the email address your XConnect account uses.
  2. Use the subject XConnect Data Deletion Request.
  3. Include the name on your account, so we can find it.

If you write from a different address, we may ask you to confirm that the account is yours before we delete anything, so that nobody can delete someone else's account. We confirm your request within [5] business days and complete it within 30 days.

Before you delete

  • It cannot be undone. You will not be able to recover your account or your attendance history. To use XConnect again, you would create a new account.
  • If you only want to stop being tracked, you do not need to delete your account. Stop detection in the app, or turn off the Bluetooth, location or microphone permissions in your phone's settings.
  • If you are an administrator, your account is removed, but the approved administrator list is managed separately by the company. Ask us if you want to be removed from it too.

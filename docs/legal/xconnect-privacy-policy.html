The short version
  • XConnect records who is physically in a room, so hosts can take attendance without roll calls.
  • To do that, your phone briefly reads nearby Bluetooth devices, nearby Wi-Fi networks, its own motion, and an inaudible sound code. It does not record conversations and it does not collect your GPS location.

  • We do not sell your data, we do not run advertising, and the app contains no analytics or advertising tools.
  • You can delete your account in the app, or by email, at any time.

3. How presence detection works

A host's phone acts as the anchor for a room. Each phone nearby broadcasts a short, changing Bluetooth code and listens for others. Every ten seconds, while detection is running, the app sends a small report of what it heard to our server: nearby codes, nearby Wi-Fi networks, its motion, and whether it heard the room's sound code. The server compares these reports. A phone that is close to the host's phone, sees a similar set of Wi-Fi networks, and heard the room's sound code is counted as in the room. The server then produces an attendance record with a confidence reading.

Detection runs only while you have started it in the app. Stopping it, leaving the room or signing out ends it.

6. Who can see it

6.1 Hosts, administrators and other attendees

  • The host of a room sees the roster of that room: names, attendance status and confidence.
  • Administrators sign in with a verified email address that is on an approved list. They can see names, emails and attendance for the rooms and sessions they manage, invite people and send notifications.
  • Other attendees cannot see your attendance, name list or email. An attendee's screen shows only their own status and a head count.

11. Automated decisions

The confidence reading and the in-room decision are produced automatically from the sensor signals described above. They do not by themselves have a legal effect on you. If you believe a record is wrong, for example you were in a room but are marked absent, contact the room's host or administrator, or us, and the record can be reviewed and corrected.

12. Security

  • Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS).
  • The app stores your sign-in session and device identifier in your phone's secure storage.
  • Administrator access requires a verified email address that is on an approved list, and is checked on every request.
  • Access to our servers and database is limited to named people, and passwords and keys are not kept in our source code.
  • No system is completely secure. If we learn of a breach that affects your data, we will notify you and the authorities as the law requires.
